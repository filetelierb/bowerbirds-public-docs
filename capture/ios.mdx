The iOS app uses the same Buckets, records, annotations, and routing concepts as macOS. Its primary navigation has three tabs: **Buckets**, **Inbox**, and **Settings**.

## Capture menu

Open **Buckets** and tap **+** to choose:

- **Quick Note** for text;
- **Camera** for a new photo;
- **Photo Library** for an existing image;
- **Record** for microphone, device audio, screen, or any supported combination.

Record uses the same source model as macOS. iOS cannot capture calls or FaceTime audio, and individual apps can withhold their device audio. Screen recording is delivered through the system broadcast surface.

The keyboard extension provides typing, Dictation, and a picker for recent records. App Intents expose selected quick actions to Shortcuts and other system surfaces.

## Share extension

The share extension accepts up to ten images in one handoff and can ingest text and images shared from Apple Notes. Shared content is imported when Bowerbirds opens. If its selected destination is no longer available, the content remains private in Unsorted.

## Filing

Depending on the device routing setting, new captures either stay in private Unsorted or are filed to the shared Triage shelf. If Bowerbirds is offline, the capture remains available locally and can sync later.

Quick Blink is available later from a filed recording. It compares a selected span locally and creates a Package from the reviewed frames; it is not an item in the capture menu.

## Editing parity

Captures can retain the original image, reviewed annotations, numbered comments, and optional Forms and answers. Visual marks remain editable in Bowerbirds.

<Info>
  Synced records can be reviewed from both iOS and macOS.
</Info>
